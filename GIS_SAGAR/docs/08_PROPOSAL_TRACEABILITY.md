## Editorial decisions

- Historical national statistics and disputed/current percentages are omitted from the short About copy rather than presented as current facts.
- Old monetary estimates, satellite-resolution aspirations and forecast economic losses are not used as current product claims.
- 'Soil texture and slope are enabled for the current assessment' preserves the requested staged-framework wording.
- The application currently uses weighted deterministic screening; do not describe it as an AI-powered predictor.
- No guarantee of increased recharge, groundwater recovery, construction fitness or carbon benefit is made.
- The author email, personal acknowledgments and institutional logos are not needed in the public About panel.
